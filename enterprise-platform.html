02 · The platform

The platform in 60 seconds

An underwriter decides whether to insure a risk, like a fleet of aircraft, a ship, or a building project, and at what price. Once the policy is live, the insurer keeps watching it, and what they see shapes the next decision. The platform covers the whole cycle.

Before the policy · Pre-bind
After the policy · Post-bind
1
Broker

Sends submission

Details arrive as emails, PDFs and spreadsheets.

2
Clearance teamSome clients

Checks the data

AI turns the pile into one record. Clients with a separate clearance team check it here. Others go straight to review.

3
Underwriter

Assesses and prices

The risk is checked against the insurer's rules and loss history, then priced. The underwriter sees how it adds to what is already written.

4
Underwriter

Quotes and binds

The accepted quote becomes a live policy.

5
Underwriter

Tracks exposure

A map shows insured value piling up in one place, like a port or a blast zone. Alerts fire when limits get close or an event happens nearby.

6
Underwriter

Manages the policy

Mid-term changes, reports, and renewal.

Renewal
Live exposure informs every new quote
  • Renewal: step 6 leads back to step 1.
  • Live exposure informs every new quote: step 5 feeds back into step 3.

Accumulation: the total an insurer could lose if one event hits one place, like a storm at a port full of insured ships.

Covered in: "One workflow for every line of business" (steps 1 to 4) · "Letting each client run the platform themselves" (admin setup)
