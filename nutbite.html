Simpler than a notebook: a store app for a first-time user

A family-run cashew business tracked every sale in notebooks and WhatsApp photos. I designed and built an app that records each sale, updates stock automatically, and gives the owners a live view of their store.

Step 03 · Ideation

They asked for a sales record. I gave them a way to run the store

Research showed me gaps the owners had not mentioned. I turned each one into a part of the app.

What they asked for

Record sales
→

What I gave them

  • Record sales
  • Low stock warnings
  • Best and slow sellers
  • Sales reports
  • A bill for every sale

  1. I spotted more gaps

    While learning how the store ran, four more problems stood out.

    • Running lowStock ran out without warning, sometimes while a customer waited.
    • Buying from memorySlow movers sat on the shelf while best sellers ran short.
    • Once a monthThe owners only had time to review the business on a weekend.
    • No billCustomers left without a proper bill.

  2. Each gap became a feature

    All of it runs from the sales the storekeeper records. She does nothing extra.

    • Low

      Low stock warnings

      Flags products before they run out.

    • Best and slow sellers

      Shows what sells and what does not.

    • WeekMonthYear

      Sales reports

      Weekly, monthly and yearly, ready any time.

    • A bill for every sale

      Printed in one step from each recorded sale.

Step 04 · Wireframes

From wireframes to decisions

I drew a simple layout (a wireframe) of every screen in FigJam first, testing each one against the storekeeper's needs before building anything.

1 / 6Click a wireframe to see it larger

Five decisions that shaped the app

  1. 01

    The catalogue came first

    Every brand, product, pack size and price, defined once. Inventory, Sales and Reports all read from it.

    WhyShe never types a product name or price again.

  2. 02

    One pattern on every screen

    The same card-and-tap interaction on Inventory and Sales.

    WhyLearn one screen, and you know them all.

  3. 03

    Web and mobile from the start

    The counter computer for the storekeeper. Phones for the owners.

    WhyShe works next to the printer. They check the store from anywhere.

  4. 04

    Owner actions behind a PIN

    Pricing, catalogue edits and reports are for owners only.

    WhyShe could not break anything that mattered.

  5. 05

    The bill comes from the sale

    Recording a sale fills in the bill on its own.

    WhyNo extra typing. Every customer leaves with a proper bill.

Step 06 · How I built it

From wireframe to a live app, built with AI

After wireframing in FigJam, I skipped the usual detailed design stage. I turned each wireframe and requirement into a prompt, built the full working app myself with Claude Design, then took it live and handed it to the client.
