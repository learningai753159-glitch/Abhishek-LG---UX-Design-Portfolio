01 · The store

A family business, run after full-time jobs.

A husband and wife run a cashew business in Kumta, Karnataka. Staff run the unit and the store day to day.

  1. Owners
  2. Manufacturing unit
  3. One store

02 · The problem

Every sale went into a notebook.

  1. A sale is made
  2. Written in a notebook
  3. Photographed
  4. Sent on WhatsApp
  • MonSent
  • TueMissed
  • WedLost
  • ThuSent
  • FriLate
  • SatMissed
  • SunSent

Where it broke

  • Reports were missed
  • Photos got lost
  • Nothing was in one place
  • The owners had to copy everything into one place by hand

The result: the store ran on trust. Whatever the staff reported, the owners had to believe. They had no control and no clear picture of their own store.

03 · The ask

A simple app to record daily sales.

The owners asked for a day-to-day sales recorder. Nothing more.

  • The storekeeperRecords every sale, every day.
  • The ownersOpen the app and see the day's sales for themselves.

04 · The users

New to computers. A notebook to beat.

  • Primary user · The storekeeperNot very tech-savvy. She uses WhatsApp, YouTube and Google Pay on her phone, and not much else. Her knowledge of computers is very basic, so the app had to be simple enough for her to use every day.
  • Secondary users · The ownersThey want to see what is happening in the store and keep track of it.

"If it takes longer than writing in the notebook, she will go back to the notebook."

05 · The idea

They asked for a sales record. I gave them a store.

  • Asked forRecord every sale digitally.
  • Also deliveredLow stock warnings, best and slow sellers, sales reports, a bill for every sale.

All of it runs from the sales she records. She does nothing extra.

06 · The decisions

One pattern. Fewest taps.

  • Catalogue firstEvery product and price defined once.
  • One card-and-tap patternLearn one screen, know them all.
  • Owner actions behind a PINShe can't break anything that matters.

07 · The build

Each wireframe and requirement became a prompt. I built the full app with Claude Design, took it live and handed it over.
