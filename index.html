More about me

I start with research and end with products people can actually use

I've spent 7 years designing for insurance technology, e-commerce and AI agent products. Most of my work is complex business software that people use all day. I care about the small details that let them get on with their job.

The path so far

  1. 2026
    NutBite (freelance project)

    I designed and built NutBite, an app that helps a family-run cashew business record sales and track stock.

  2. 2023
    Staff UX Designer, Concirrus

    I grew the design team from one to four. We designed AI-enabled tools for underwriters, the people who decide what to insure and at what price, across four types of insurance, including marine, aviation and construction.

  3. 2020
    UX Designer, CoreValue Technologies

    I owned design for four live products at once, across connected devices (IoT), insurance, e-commerce and web, with clients in India, the UK and the US.

  4. 2019
    UI Designer, StoreKing

    My first design role. I designed responsive interfaces for an e-commerce technology business.

  5. 2018
    The switch

    I trained in UX design and took on freelance projects to move into design full time.

  6. 2017
    Talent Acquisition Executive, TalentXO (formerly CrewKarma)

    I started in hiring, not design. It taught me to listen closely and keep many people moving in one direction.
